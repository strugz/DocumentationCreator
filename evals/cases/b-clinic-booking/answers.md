Interview answers (from the clinic's operations manager):

A. Purpose: Today bookings are taken by phone and written in a paper logbook. Double
   bookings and no-shows are common. Success = fewer no-shows and no double bookings.
B. Users: Patients (public), Receptionists (2), Doctors (4), and one Administrator who
   manages accounts and clinic settings.
C. Features: Must — online booking by patients, receptionist booking management
   (create, reschedule, cancel), SMS reminders the day before, doctor daily schedule.
   Should — monthly no-show report. Won't — online payments (patients pay at the clinic).
D. Workflows: patient books a slot; receptionist reschedules a booking by phone request;
   doctor checks today's schedule each morning.
E. Data: patients (name, mobile number, birth date), doctors, appointment slots,
   appointments. No existing data to import.
F. Platform: web, must work on phones. Hosting: not decided yet. Sign-in: own accounts.
   UI languages: English and Filipino. No offline use.
G. Integrations: an SMS gateway — no preference which provider.
H. Constraints: go-live deadline 2027-03-31. Budget: skip. Team: skip.
   Patient data is personal health information.
I. Context: we prefer not to name the clinic in documents yet. Approver: skip.
